## PXD001101: completed pilot and scientific findings

### Design and executed settings

The pilot is `H1975_pST_BR1`: twelve Orbitrap Elite/HCD fractions from one biological mixture. Its published design has 36 file/channel rows, twelve distinct `.mzML` names, fractions 1–12, one fraction group, and labels 1/2/3 each represented twelve times.

| Channel | Chemistry | Condition | Native comparison with reference 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L / 1 | Unlabeled K/R | Serum-starved control | L/M = control/EGF |
| M / 2 | Lys4 / Arg6 | EGF stimulation | Executed reference |
| H / 3 | Lys8 / Arg10 | Erlotinib followed by EGF | H/M = drug+EGF/EGF |

Executed search settings include Trypsin, two missed cleavages, 7 ppm precursor tolerance, the corrected phospho/other modification list, explicit Comet fragment setting `0.02` Da and twelve threads. The Da value is a documented binning choice, not a mathematical conversion of the reported 20 ppm. MS1 used reference channel 2, fraction aggregation `sum`, minimum ratio count 2 and no ratio normalization.

The pilot has **one biological replicate**. Fractions, channels and converter-generated `MSstats_BioReplicate` source numbers do not create additional independent experiments. Equal input protein mixing is not a known 1:1 phosphopeptide benchmark.

### Ratio distributions and interpretation

| Population | n | Median L/M | Median H/M |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| All quantified modified peptides | 3,589 | 1.324 | 1.001 |
| Phosphorylated identities | 1,536 | 1.404 | 1.000 |
| Identities without phosphorylation | 2,053 | 1.285 | 1.003 |
| Protein groups with accepted ratios | 656 | 1.325 | 1.001 |

These are descriptive distributions, not significance tests, mixing-accuracy estimates or proof of absent drug response. Individual peptides can respond while the population median remains near one. Obtain M/L by inverting individual L/M estimates; inverting only a summary median is not an independently calculated M/L median.

Protein rollups combine enriched peptide evidence and can mix modified/unmodified peptides. They do not measure total-protein abundance, site occupancy or confidently localized phosphosites.

### Findings requiring investigation

1. **Protein q-value boundary:** ten exported top-level protein/group rows have reported q-values above the requested `proteinFDR=0.01`, up to `0.010544815465729`. The retained review identifies nine single-protein rows and one indistinguishable group. This was independently reproduced from the QPX-run mzTab. Inspect filtering, score assignment and export behavior before claiming every exported protein/group score is at or below 1%; do not silently alter the preserved outputs.
2. **Phosphorylation coverage and support:** 42.8% of quantified identities carry phosphorylation. This describes the selected quantified population, not enrichment efficiency. Compare BR1-specific deposited evidence, search modifications, missed cleavages, isotope/PTM modification budgets and identification-to-feature losses. The previously inspected CometAdapter default permits five variable modifications per peptide, including isotope modifications; any sensitivity comparison should be explicit and separate from the baseline.
3. **Localization and biological inference:** PSM/protein filtering does not establish correct phosphosite assignment. No localization/site-level error-control or validated site aggregation is implemented. One biological unit cannot support replicated treatment tests or a sensitive-versus-resistant cell-line comparison.
4. **Database and acquisition scope:** the reviewed UniProt FASTA differs from the original RefSeq 49 plus contaminants database. This pilot validates execution on the Elite branch only. The full study's 73 Elite and 37 XL files require different search policies that current global fragment/instrument overrides cannot represent together.

## Annotation audit: updated readiness and remaining gaps

The October 12 audit freshly validated the PXD001101 full SDRF and corrected pilot against their declared ms-proteomics template with ontology checking enabled. This supersedes the earlier pilot's structural-only validation limitation for those later validator executions; it does not mean the older processing container performed ontology checks.

Expanded human/cell-lines validation failed because `characteristics[cellosaurus accession]`, `characteristics[age]` and `characteristics[sex]` are absent, with cell-line term warnings. Broader applicable-template validation therefore remains incomplete. The audit found agreement for all 110 bounded instrument-header strings; this was header-prefix string extraction, not vendor-API or scan-level acquisition validation.

Disposable mutation probes showed that the shared converter checker can accept incorrect URI, enzyme, label and acquisition-method fields. These are demonstrated checker coverage gaps, not evidence that the corresponding canonical fields are wrong. Extend comparisons and meaningful regression tests before relying on the checker for those fields.

Supporting annotation work covers primary-cell turnover datasets PXD008511/512/513 and resistance study PXD020108. Generic instrument strings leave some primary-cell Plus-specific assignments insufficiently corroborated. PXD020108's later correction resolved 300 instrument assignments, but its formal validation still fails on unresolved biological replicates/fractions and its conversion replaces 32 unknown fractions with 1. It remains unsuitable as a fully verified analysis design.
